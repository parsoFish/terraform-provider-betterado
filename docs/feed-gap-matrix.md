### Priority writable gaps for future WIs

1. **`betterado_feed.upstream_enabled`** — single bool; low risk, high value. Upstream enablement is a core feed configuration.
2. **`betterado_feed.upstream_sources`** — complex nested block; enables ADO upstream proxying. Dedicated WI warranted.
3. **`betterado_feed.description`** — straightforward string; low effort.
4. **`betterado_feed.hide_deleted_package_versions`** — single bool; low risk.
5. **`betterado_feed.badges_enabled`** — single bool; low risk.
6. **`betterado_feed.default_view_id`** — UUID; needs view lookup support.
7. **`data.betterado_feed` computed exports** — surface `upstream_enabled`, `description`, `badges_enabled`, `hide_deleted_package_versions`, `default_view_id`, `upstream_sources` as computed attributes.
